# WhiteCat 2.1 gameplay sprites

These images and skin.ini were copied from the user-provided WhiteCat 2.1 skin
for replay snapshot rendering. Original skin author: cyperdark.

- Author: https://osu.ppy.sh/users/9893708
- Original skin: https://ck1t.ru/s-WhiteCat%202.1%20(CK)
- Only the used gameplay PNGs and skin.ini are bundled; @2x images are preferred,
  with SD images retained only where the original skin has no @2x counterpart.
  Unused sprites, animation sequences, audio and menu assets are omitted.
- hit0, hit50 and hit100 use the original @2x animation frame 0, renamed to a static PNG.
- Original canvas dimensions and transparent margins are preserved, including
  the blank lower halves of judgement images. Judgements are positioned by the centre
  of their non-transparent bounds. Rendering divides @2x dimensions by two to preserve
  logical sprite sizes and font spacing.
